# Qualification policy

Qualification is a set of visible gates, not a single score. It will cover out-of-sample and walk-forward stability, periods and instruments, drawdown, turnover, exposure, campaign-wide validation trade count, parameter and cost sensitivity, delayed execution, missing data, regimes, profit concentration, benchmarks, and search volume.

`qualification-gates-v1` is the approved policy. The strict loader rejects malformed proposals and mismatches between proposal status and gate approval flags. Missing or invalid metrics fail their gates, and disqualifying failures reject the experiment. Failed experiments cannot qualify. Holdout metrics remain hidden until a completed holdout receives an explicit logged qualification event. Any threshold change requires its own human-reviewed change and evidence; it cannot accompany strategy or parameter changes.

The approved benchmark, risk, exposure, concentration, sensitivity, regime-coverage, activity, and search-volume thresholds are recorded in `config/research/qualification-proposal.json`. They are project policy choices, not universal financial claims. Approval alone does not qualify a candidate or authorize holdout access.

Cost and delayed-fill sensitivity results remain separate candidates and cannot alter qualification thresholds. Candidate comparisons show each metric, failure, and missing result directly; they do not rank candidates with a composite score.

A qualification evidence manifest names each candidate's base validation folds, matched fixed-weight folds, doubled-cost variants, delayed-fill variants, and parameter neighbors. The evaluator loads only completed validation records from the named campaign that carry controlled-run provenance and exactly one report location and SHA-256 fingerprint. Manual and legacy completions cannot enter qualification. It verifies campaign, strategy, period, parent, dataset, universe, parameter, cost, and execution relationships before aggregation. It sums fills across the predeclared base-validation folds for the campaign-wide trade-count gate; return, Sharpe, drawdown, regime, and concentration gates continue to apply within each fold. It then writes a content-addressed report containing all source experiment IDs, aggregate metrics, gate results, and fingerprints. Missing, mislabeled, cross-campaign, or inconsistent evidence stops evaluation.

The Rapid-002 candidate manifest may also name exactly Stress A and Stress B as combined-cost-and-delay variants. Both must link to the named base fold, preserve source, strategy, dataset, universe, parameters, period, and seed, and match their exact cost and execution model versions. Their returns and same-base return retentions enter four visible disqualifying gates. This candidate-specific addition does not change the 17 approved daily gates or historical manifest fingerprints.

A holdout-run authorization can be stored only after the system rebuilds evidence from the registry and obtains `qualified` from an approved proposal with every gate approved and passing. The authorization binds one candidate ID, code revision, strategy version and family, parameters, cost and execution models, dataset and universe provenance, and the end of validation. A holdout must match those fields, name the qualified candidate as its parent, and begin after validation. Creating it consumes the authorization in the same transaction. Only then may the controlled runner read the exact holdout range. A load or simulation failure remains failed evidence and does not restore the authorization. The runner does not return holdout metrics or write a readable report. The candidate, manifest, proposal, and source experiment set can authorize at most one holdout even if campaign bookkeeping later changes the evidence fingerprint. A completed holdout can receive only one logged read event. A separate paper authorization revalidates the same evidence and also binds the account, reviewed risk configuration, reviewer, reason, and expiry; it grants no broker authority by itself.

`experiment review-holdout` refuses an unapproved proposal before logging access. For an approved
proposal bound to the exact holdout campaign, it records one reviewer, reason, and event ID, reveals
the protected metrics to the evaluator, applies every gate, and stores a proposal-bound review
fingerprint. An exact replay can finish an interrupted review; another event or changed authority
fails closed.

Report schema v2 calculates qualification metrics from the last equity point in each completed session; the detailed per-symbol ledger remains unchanged. Annualized volatility and the Sharpe ratio use 252 sessions and a zero reference rate. Gross exposure is long market value divided by equity. Period concentration is the share of all positive session profit supplied by the five largest positive sessions. Instrument concentration is the largest positive instrument profit divided by total positive instrument profit after fills and commissions. SPY close-to-close returns classify up and down sessions; strategy returns are compounded separately within each class.

Parameter-neighborhood runs are separate validation candidates linked to the base candidate. They consume campaign budget and may reveal fragility, but they cannot select a new parameter from validation results. Missing regime classes or undefined risk and concentration values fail any gate that requires them.

## Intraday research gates

`intraday-qualification-policy-v1` is separate from approved daily qualification. Its initial thresholds are reviewed research parameters, not financial validation. It requires controlled registry provenance, complete fixed-budget accounting, visible sample, activity, drawdown, concentration, session-boundary, early-close, cost-stress, whole-bar-delay, and lineage evidence. The required stress set contains increased and materially harsher costs plus `+1` and `+2` bar delays. The assessor verifies completed controlled-run records against their one stored report path and fingerprint, then lists every candidate status in the campaign. Missing, failed, rejected, malformed, mismatched, or unaccounted evidence cannot disappear or pass its gate.

Campaign V2 failed all 12 base research qualification groups. That result does not weaken or reinterpret the thresholds. Its delayed-fill variants remain valid evidence under their frozen execution model even though the postmortem found that pending orders confounded latency with accepted target cadence.

The V3 report adds state-transition, turnover-per-session, and paired zero-cost diagnostic metrics. The V1 assessor rejects `intraday-backtest-report-v2`. `intraday-v3-qualification-binding-v1` separately binds the unchanged policy to exact V3 reports. It reads gate metrics only from `realistic.metrics`, checks the realistic result, zero-cost result, semantic trace, dataset, configuration, execution contract, report integrity, exact five-role lineage, reviewed source evidence, and a fingerprinted 60-record campaign ledger. Every completed record must bind one immutable controlled report to its review and per-candidate source binding; every failed record must retain a reason and identity. Caller-selected stress IDs are not accepted: the registered path derives all five roles from the sealed plan. The zero-cost object has no gate metrics and cannot satisfy return or stress gates. Passing research gates still leaves research qualification, protected holdout, paper, broker-write, and live authority false. No turnover gate is active or proposed, and no V3 report has been assessed.

Intraday parameter neighbors remain linked children of one frozen candidate. They can expose fragility but cannot select a replacement parameter from validation output. `intraday-qualification-evidence-v1` has no path to daily holdout authorization, paper authorization, broker mutation, or promotion. A future protected intraday holdout requires separate policy and authority review.
